# Choose what the map contributes

Author a map for the interpretation it gives the reader.
That can matter over a handful of notes or a large cluster.
Size alone does not decide.

[[map::au-base-types]] is an open synthesis kind derived from idea.
Choose a form for the reader's task:

- An argument showing how claims relate
- Orientation explaining why notes belong together
- A comparison or reading route
- Another synthesis the reader needs beyond membership

**Give a home a reading route**

A home should let a newcomer choose what to open and understand why.
Lead with the subject in plain language and a few existing notes that unlock it.
Group onward routes by the questions or ideas readers pursue, with a reason for each destination.
Let the available material determine the structure: a fresh home may need only one useful note and an open question; a grown home can lead into maps of distinct regions.
Do not invent empty rooms or impose a fixed number of sections.

A list of types, folders, or query expressions describes storage without necessarily orienting a reader.
A query written in a code span is an instruction to an operator, not a working navigation control.
Keep useful live views alongside the reading route; do not substitute query syntax for links to the knowledge already present.

Before finishing, follow the proposed start and one onward route as a newcomer.
Can the reader reach the substance without knowing the schema or running a tool, and distinguish established understanding from unfinished work?
Repair the route where that fails.

**Compute membership when a relation defines it**

A query or computed index can maintain a defined set or tree more reliably than a hand-kept list.
It can coexist with authored synthesis.

Keep ownership clear.
Regeneration should preserve human interpretation.
A generated list should remain distinguishable from a reviewed argument.

**Explain the arrangement**

Show why links belong at the level the reader needs.
A shared heading or explanation can make the relationship clear without an annotation on every link.
An exhaustive list alone does not supply synthesis.

Use a map subtype when a recurring form needs a contract or a consumer distinguishes it.
A visual arrangement alone does not justify another type.
See [[add-structure-when-queried]].

Name the understanding the reader should gain beyond a list of members.
Author that interpretation.
Use the available query or computed view when accurate enumeration is the whole task.
